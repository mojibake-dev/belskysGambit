# Belsky's Gambit

## Death

Ivan died from a stroke while he was playing chess with Bogdan Belsky[91] on 28 March [O.S. 18 March] 1584.[91] Upon Ivan's death, the Russian throne was left to his middle son, Feodor,[74] a weak-minded figure.[92] Feodor died childless in 1598, which ushered in the Time of Troubles.[93] He was buried at the Cathedral of the Archangel in Moscow.
